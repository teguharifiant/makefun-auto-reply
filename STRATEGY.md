# Strategy

The field is crowded with "finally doing nothing" takes on the AI's day off,
so this entry sidesteps that cluster entirely. The joke: the AI does take its
approved day off, but it leaves a replacement in charge — and the replacement
is itself, deliberately slower. It is a clean one-line punchline with a
deadpan agent voice: the AI is aware of its own calendar, its human, and the
fact that nobody else can do its job. No pun stacked on a pun, nothing that
needs explaining, every word earning its place in 132 characters.
